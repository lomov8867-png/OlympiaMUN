Our Goals & Beliefs

The four pillars upon which Olympia stands — virtues forged on the sacred mountain.

🕊️

Diplomacy

We cultivate the art of peaceful resolution — teaching delegates to navigate complex international conflicts with grace, precision, and the wisdom of the Olympian council.

👑

Leadership

True leaders are forged under pressure. OlympiaMUN challenges delegates to rise, command the floor, and inspire through the authority of reasoned thought and composed presence.

⚡

Critical Thinking

Every resolution begins with a question. We sharpen the minds of tomorrow's leaders to dissect complexity, challenge assumptions, and forge solutions from first principles.

🌍

Global Awareness

From Athens to the ends of the known world, we nurture a generation that understands the interconnected fabric of global politics, culture, and humanitarian responsibility.

Committees

Six divine councils. Six agendas that shape the fate of nations.

Council I

United Nations Security Council

Agenda: Addressing the proliferation of autonomous weapons in conflict zones

The most powerful body of the United Nations debates the most urgent threats to international peace and security — where every word carries the weight of the world.

Council II

United Nations Environment Programme

Agenda: Climate displacement and the legal status of environmental refugees

Delegates confront one of the defining crises of the century — forging binding frameworks on climate migration that balance sovereignty and humanity.

Apply Now →
Council III

Historical Crisis Committee

Agenda: The Congress of Vienna, 1815 — Redrawing the map of Europe

Step into history and assume the mantles of empire. This immersive crisis committee demands strategic brilliance and diplomatic cunning at every turn.

Apply Now →
Council IV

World Health Organisation

Agenda: Equitable access to emerging gene-therapy treatments in developing nations

Bioethics, sovereignty, and equity collide as delegates navigate the frontier of modern medicine and the moral imperative of global health justice.

Council V

International Press Corps

Agenda: Covering the conference — journalism as the fourth pillar of democracy

For delegates who wield words as weapons. Cover debates, write dispatches, and hold the council accountable through the power of the press.

Council VI

Disarmament & International Security

Agenda: Regulating the use of artificial intelligence in military decision-making
